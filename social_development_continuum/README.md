## 📌 Normative Alignment & Sovereign Legal Harmony
Every framework in this continuum is authored in strict compliance with the **Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan (1973)** (specifically Fundamental Rights, Articles 9, 14, 25, 25A, 34, 37, and 38) and relevant federal/provincial statutes, harmonized with universal human rights compacts:
1. **UN Sustainable Development Goals (Agenda 2030):** Primary mapping to SDGs 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 13, 16, and 17.
2. **International Treaties:** CEDAW, UN Convention on the Rights of the Child (CRC), and Paris Climate Agreement.
3. **Statutory National Acts:** NCSW Act 2012, Protection Against Harassment Act 2010/2022, BISP Act 2010, Juvenile Justice System Act 2018, and National Water Policy 2018.
